Our Story

A family kitchen serving real halal flavour

Donair Choices Cafe was born from a simple idea: bring Calgary generous portions of authentic, 100% halal comfort food made the way family makes it — fresh, flavourful and full of heart. From our slow-marinated donair spit to hand-shaken bubble tea, everything is prepared to order.

Tucked into Beddington Towne Centre in NW Calgary, we've become a neighbourhood favourite for quick lunches, family dinners and late-night cravings alike.
